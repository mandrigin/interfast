Interfast is a minimal, beautiful intermittent fasting timer that respects your privacy.

FEATURES

Timer & Tracking
- Accurate fasting timer with visual progress ring
- Multiple protocols: 16:8, 18:6, 20:4, 23:1 (OMAD), or create your own
- History tracking with calendar view
- Streak tracking and statistics

Delightful Experience
- Distinctive dot-matrix design inspired by Nothing Phone
- Haptic feedback for satisfying interactions
- Shake-to-start for zero-friction quick start
- Smart motivational notifications

Privacy First
- All data stored locally on your device
- No accounts, no cloud sync, no tracking
- Export your data anytime (JSON or CSV)
- Open source and auditable

Widgets
- 2x2 Compact: Quick glance timer
- 4x1 Banner: Horizontal status bar
- 4x2 Dashboard: Full stats at a glance

Accessible
- Full TalkBack support
- Large text scaling
- High contrast mode
- Minimum 48dp touch targets

DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

Interfast synthesizes four design influences:
- Nothing Phone: Dot matrix typography, glyph interface patterns
- Swiss Design: Rigid grid systems, typographic hierarchy
- Bauhaus: Geometric forms, functional color
- Cypherpunk: Monospace data, terminal aesthetics

The result is an interface that feels both technical and approachable.

OPEN SOURCE

Interfast is free and open source software, licensed under Apache-2.0.
No ads. No premium features. No data collection.

Your fasting journey is yours alone.
