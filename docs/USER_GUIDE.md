# Interfast User Guide

Welcome to Interfast, your minimal and beautiful intermittent fasting companion.

## Getting Started

### Your First Fast

1. **Open Interfast** - You'll see the timer screen with a large circular progress ring
2. **Select a Protocol** - Tap the protocol name (e.g., "16:8") in the top-left to choose your fasting schedule
3. **Start Fasting** - Tap the red "START FAST" button to begin
4. **Track Your Progress** - Watch the dot ring fill as you progress through your fast

## Fasting Protocols

Interfast supports several popular intermittent fasting protocols:

### 16:8 (Default)
- **Fasting**: 16 hours
- **Eating**: 8 hours
- **Best for**: Beginners, daily practice

### 18:6
- **Fasting**: 18 hours
- **Eating**: 6 hours
- **Best for**: Intermediate fasters

### 20:4 (Warrior Diet)
- **Fasting**: 20 hours
- **Eating**: 4 hours
- **Best for**: Experienced fasters

### 23:1 (OMAD)
- **Fasting**: 23 hours
- **Eating**: 1 hour
- **Best for**: Advanced practitioners, one meal a day

### Custom
Create your own protocol with fasting hours from 12-23.

## History & Calendar

The History screen shows your fasting journey:

### Calendar View
- **Green dot (●)**: Completed fast that day
- **Amber dot (◐)**: Partial fast (started but ended early)
- **Gray dot (○)**: No fasting recorded

### Session List
Below the calendar, you'll see your recent fasting sessions with:
- Start and end times
- Duration achieved
- Completion status

## Statistics

Track your progress with detailed statistics:

### This Week
- Bar chart showing daily fasting hours
- Weekly completion percentage
- Average hours per day

### Records
- **Current Streak**: Consecutive days completed
- **Longest Streak**: Your best streak ever
- **Total Hours**: Lifetime fasting hours
- **Completed Fasts**: Total number of completed fasts

### Completion Rate
Your overall success rate across all fasts.

## Notifications

Interfast can notify you at key milestones:

### Milestone Alerts
Get notified when you reach:
- 25% complete
- 50% complete (halfway!)
- 75% complete
- 100% complete (eating window opens)

### Enabling Notifications
1. Go to Settings
2. Enable "Notifications"
3. Enable "Milestone Alerts"
4. Grant notification permission when prompted

## Tips for Success

### Starting Out
1. **Begin with 16:8** - It's manageable for most people
2. **Stay hydrated** - Water, black coffee, and tea are fasting-friendly
3. **Build the habit** - Consistency matters more than perfection

### Maintaining Streaks
- Start your fast after dinner to sleep through the hardest hours
- Plan your eating window around your schedule
- If you break early, still log it - partial fasts count!

### Common Questions

**Q: Does the timer work when the app is closed?**
A: Yes! The timer runs in the background and will continue accurately.

**Q: Can I pause my fast?**
A: Currently, you can only end a fast early. A pause feature may come in future updates.

**Q: What counts as breaking a fast?**
A: Generally, any caloric intake breaks a fast. Water, black coffee, and plain tea are usually considered acceptable.

**Q: How accurate is the timer?**
A: The timer is accurate to within seconds over a 24-hour period.

## Troubleshooting

### Timer Not Updating
- Ensure the app isn't being killed by battery optimization
- Add Interfast to your battery optimization whitelist

### Notifications Not Working
1. Check Settings > Notifications are enabled
2. Verify Android notification permissions
3. Ensure Do Not Disturb isn't blocking alerts

### Widget Not Updating
Widgets update every 15 minutes by default. For immediate updates, tap the widget to open the app.

## Privacy

Interfast stores all data locally on your device. No data is sent to external servers. Your fasting history is yours alone.
